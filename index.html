How Sinus Infection Therapy Works, Step by Step

From blocked to open, in one in-office visit.

Balloon sinuplasty is the procedure behind our name — here's what actually happens during it, at a glance.

Step 01

Diagnose the blockage

A precise exam pinpoints exactly which sinus passage is inflamed or blocked, causing infection, pressure, or congestion.

Step 02

Gently open the passage

A small balloon catheter is placed and gently expanded to widen the sinus opening — no cutting or tissue removal.

Step 03

Restore normal drainage

The balloon is removed, leaving a wider, restructured passage that drains and ventilates normally — most patients recover in days.
